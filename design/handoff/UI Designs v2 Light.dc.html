Nash Equilibrium Math & Logic
BRIEF A Nash equilibrium is a state where no player can improve their outcome by changing strategy alone, given everyone else's strategy stays fixed — the foundational concept for predicting how self-interested parties behave when outcomes depend on each other.
The Prisoner's Dilemma is the standard proof of this — the equilibrium is worse for both players than if they'd cooperated.
