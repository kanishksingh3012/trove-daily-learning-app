DESIGN SYSTEM · v2 Daily Learning Companion One monochrome-primary system in two modes. Dark mode is the reference build; light mode is derived from it token for token. The accent is neutral by default and can be switched to one of three colour themes in Settings.

02 · ACCENT THEMES Four accents, switched in Settings → Appearance. Neutral is the default: it renders as off-white on dark and as ink on light, so the accent is always the highest-contrast value in the mode. Coloured themes keep white text on the fill in both modes.

07 · RULES
Do One accent per screen. The filled accent marks the single primary action; everything else is outline or text. Show selection with the accent fill, never with colour alone at the same weight as neighbouring text. Keep category colour for identity only — folders, tags, and the domain colour picker. Give every write a visible confirmation, and every failed write a visible error. Keep the toast and selection tooltip dark in both modes.
Don't Two filled accent elements competing in one region. Accent text on an accent fill, or #FFF text where the token is --text. Theme-coloured category tags or folder icons. Gradients or coloured backgrounds behind body copy; surfaces stay neutral. Hit targets under 44 px, or body copy under 11 px at frame scale.
